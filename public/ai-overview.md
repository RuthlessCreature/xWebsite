# Zhuhai Xiaodu Intelligent Technology

## Business overview

Zhuhai Xiaodu Intelligent Technology describes itself as a Zhuhai-based industrial automation and intelligent manufacturing project delivery company serving customers in China and overseas.

Its published service areas include industrial robotics and automation system integration, machine vision and inspection, production data platforms, automated sampling, laboratory automation, custom equipment, production-line integration and project engineering.

Project scope, suitability, schedule and pricing depend on the production process, site conditions, interfaces, safety requirements and acceptance criteria. The public site does not establish a guaranteed outcome, customer result, certification or named partnership unless a page provides supporting evidence.
